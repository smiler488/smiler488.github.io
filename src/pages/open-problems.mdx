## Making crop state projectable

**Question.** How do we move from a crop that is observable and explainable (Layers I–II) to one whose growth can be projected under environment and management scenarios, with decision risk quantified (Layer III)?

**Starting point.** Coupling structure–radiation–photosynthesis–growth processes with data assimilation. This is the focus of the current postdoc.

## Leaf optics beyond the measured domain

**Question.** Can trait-based BRDF prediction hold across genotypes, environments, developmental stages and water status?

**Why it is open.** The [BRDF model](/research/brdf-traits) was built from 270 data entries across four species. Predictions outside the measured trait and wavelength ranges need new validation.

## From simulated light fields to field outcomes

**Question.** When leaf optical diversity changes the simulated light field inside a canopy, does that translate into measurable differences in the field?

**Why it is open.** The ray-tracing results show changes in simulated light distribution; on their own they do not demonstrate yield gains.

## Cross-modal registration over time

**Question.** How should hyperspectral, LiDAR, RGB and thermal observations of the same plants be registered and analysed over time?

**Why it is open.** In the [MCTP workspace](/research/mctp-workspace), cross-modal registration and temporal analysis are not yet automatic capabilities.
